# Charter

1. **No owner.** Commonhold belongs to no person or company. The organisation has at least three owners, decisions
   are made in the open, and anyone can fork.
2. **No game files.** Only character data, signatures and listings travel. Players use their own clients and files.
3. **No money.** No coins, tokens, donations for perks, paid placement or tradeable characters, anywhere.
4. **Home is the source of truth.** The host controls its own world, and the player approves what comes home.
5. **Privacy by default.** A key is the only identity. Relays hide home addresses. Nothing is harvested.
6. **Clean provenance.** Shared code targets stock open-source cores. Nothing decompiled, recompiled or leaked goes
   into shared code, listings or links, and the network never requires a modified client binary.
7. **Fair listing.** Placement follows published criteria any world can meet.
8. **Credit by default.** Every piece carries its author's name, everywhere it appears.
9. **Each door keeps its name.** Projects that join stay independent and branded; Commonhold never absorbs them.
