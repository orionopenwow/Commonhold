# Your folder is yours; shared work is reviewed

## What we decide
- `members/<you>/` ships as soon as the guard check passes, with no review needed.
- Touching someone else's folder needs their approval.
- `joint/`, `wiki/` and new members need 2 other members' approval.
- `spec/` needs 3, including the section's steward.
- How the workspace itself works needs 2 plus a steward code-owner review.

## Why
Everyone keeps full control of their own work and nobody waits on anybody, while shared decisions stay shared.
