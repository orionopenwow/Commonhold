# How to join

1. **Say hello** in the Discord or open a GitHub Discussion, and say what you'd like to work on.
2. **Add your folder:** create `members/<your-handle>/member.yml` (copy one that's there) in a pull request.
   Two members approve it, and from then on that folder is yours.
3. **Ship your work:** put anything in your folder and run `python tools/ch.py ship -m "what changed"`, or on
   Windows double-click `Ship my folder.bat`. It merges itself once the guard check passes.
4. **Propose shared changes:** for the roadmap, decisions, wiki or spec, open a pull request. The guard tells you
   whose approval it needs.

Ground rules: no game files, no secrets, no money, clean provenance, and sign off your commits (`git commit -s`).
