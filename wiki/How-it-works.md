# How it works

Five ways to play, from just you to everyone:

1. **Solo:** your world and characters live on your own PC. A launcher like Leyline sets it up in one click.
2. **Session world:** open your world to friends for an evening, even on a laptop. Their progress goes home with them
   when the session ends.
3. **Visit:** take your own character to a friend's world for a while, then come home with what both worlds allow.
   Your home world keeps the original; the host only proposes what changed, and you approve it.
4. **Live together:** stay on your own server and play live with linked servers. Other players appear in your world,
   and parties, chat and trade work across servers.
5. **Big worlds:** always-on realms take part when they choose to, and set their own rules for visitors.

**Your character can't be taken away.** Your launcher always keeps the latest signed copy of every character. A few
community "waystations" keep a fingerprint of each version (never the character itself), so another world can check
that your copy is real and up to date. If your server closes, you can move your character to any world that accepts
newcomers.

**Nothing to buy, nothing to download from us.** Only character data, signatures and listings travel. You use your
own game client and files. There are no coins, tokens or paid perks anywhere in the network.
