# Glossary

- **World:** a server someone runs, with its own database and rules.
- **Home world:** where your character lives. It's the source of truth for that character.
- **Passport:** a signed, portable snapshot of a character.
- **Charter:** a world's published rules for visitors.
- **League:** a group of worlds whose charters trust each other.
- **Customs:** the step that decides what a visitor may bring in and take home.
- **Vault:** where things that can't travel are kept safe at home.
- **Visit record:** the host's signed account of what happened during a visit.
- **Waystation:** a community node that keeps append-only fingerprints of passport versions.
- **Re-home:** moving a character to a new home after its world closes.
- **Door:** a launcher, list or tool that lets people into the network (Leyline, CoreCraft, Project Eternal).
- **Steward:** the member who approves changes to one section of the spec.
