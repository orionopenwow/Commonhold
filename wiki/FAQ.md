# FAQ

**Who owns Commonhold?** Nobody. It's an open specification in a neutral organisation, maintained by a small group
of builders and hosts who each keep their own projects.

**Can someone copy my character to cheat?** A copy is signed by your home world and versioned. An edited copy fails
the signature check, and an old copy fails the fingerprint check at the waystations.

**Can I sell my character?** No. Characters are bound to your key, gold never travels, and selling isn't something
the network supports.

**Does my server have to accept visitors?** No. Every world decides who and what it accepts, through its charter.

**Do I need Discord?** No. Everything that matters is in the repository: the spec, decisions and this wiki.

**Is AI used?** Some members use AI tools for drafts, code and tests. Commits made with help say so (`Assisted-by:`),
and people approve every shared change.

**What about my privacy?** A key is the only identity. No emails or IP addresses travel in any record, relays hide
home addresses, and each world you visit sees a different key unless you choose to share one.
